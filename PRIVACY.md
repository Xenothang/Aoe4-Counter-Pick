# Privacy

AoE4 Counter Pick does **not** collect, store or transmit personal data, and has no analytics or tracking.

- **What it reads:** the draft page on `aoe2cm.net` (which civilizations have been picked) — locally, in your browser.
- **What it requests:** public matchup statistics and the patch/rank filter lists from `aoe4world.com`. These requests contain no information about you or your draft; they only contain the selected patch and rank filter. Requests are made without cookies.
- **What it stores:** your settings, panel positions, a cache of the downloaded matchup data and, per draft, the draft ID with the map and civ names shown on its page (the 20 most recent drafts) plus the recently picked map names and the latest civ pool, in the browser's local extension storage (`storage.local`). This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the extension.
